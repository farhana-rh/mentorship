About the Program

Bridging academic learning and professional readiness

Women remain significantly underrepresented in engineering and technology fields. The IEEE NSU SB WIE Affinity Group created the WIE Mentorship Program to give female undergraduate students at NSU structured, sustained support — not a one-time event, but a relationship that grows across a full academic year.

Mentees are paired with experienced mentors — alumni, industry professionals, senior students, and faculty — who offer guidance, encouragement, and a real look into life in STEM. In the spirit of this year's IWD theme, Give To Gain, mentors give their time and experience, and mentees gain direction, confidence, and clarity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you apply.

Any female undergraduate student currently enrolled at North South University can apply as a mentee, regardless of department or year of study.

The program runs for one full academic year — from recruitment and mentor matching at the start, through regular mentoring, a mid-year check-in, and a closing ceremony at the end.

After registration, the WIE organizing committee reviews each mentee's field of interest, goals, and preferred meeting style, then pairs them with a compatible mentor — typically in small groups of one mentor to two or three mentees. Matches are announced at the kickoff event.

Pairs typically meet bi-weekly or monthly, depending on mutual availability — with each session recommended to run about 45–60 minutes. On top of individual meetings, the WIE Affinity Group also hosts periodic group sessions and guest speaker events for all participants.
